# Design System: DecentralExchange

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Precision Instrument"**

DecentralExchange is built like an instrument, not a dashboard template. Balances and prices are the truth of every screen, so they get the size, the weight and the motion; everything around them recedes into material. The ground is Apple's neutral grey, content sits on white (graphite in dark mode) surfaces lifted by one soft shadow, and the only chroma on a resting screen is the logo's indigo on whatever the holder is about to press or has selected. Buy and sell speak in system green and red, and nothing else is allowed to borrow them.

The craft bar is Apple Wallet and Stocks, macOS System Settings and Uniswap's swap card, played at full fidelity: large titles, grouped inset lists with hairlines that start past the avatar, segmented controls whose selection rides on a raised thumb, a translucent top bar content scrolls beneath, and digits that roll to their new value. The same system runs across the desktop and mobile app shells, in light and dark, in 17 languages. Density is comfortable rather than terminal-like: an 8px rhythm, 44px tap targets, 24px card padding.

Motion is one physical system. A single spring (stiffness 520, damping 38, mass 0.8) drives every sliding selection, sheet, toast and press, and Apple's curve `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` drives every tween. Presses answer with scale (0.97) and tint, never with an ink ripple. The component kit in `src/components/premium/` ports 21st.dev components into styled-components and motion; each file credits its origin.

**Scope exception, onboarding:** the landing page and the sign-in, sign-up, import, restore and Ledger flows deliberately keep the pre-redesign look. `LegacyOnboardingTheme` (in `src/components/legacy/`) wraps those routes and serves the older system from a frozen snapshot in `src/styles/legacy/`. Nothing in this document applies to them. Don't extend the snapshot; retire it when onboarding is redesigned.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Apple-neutral ground (light `#f5f5f7`, dark `#000000`) with white or graphite surfaces on top.
- One accent, indigo, reserved for primary action, current selection, focus and links.
- Depth is a soft two-layer shadow in light mode and a 1px hairline in dark mode, never both on one surface.
- System font stack (SF Pro on Apple platforms, self-hosted Inter Variable elsewhere); semibold titles, regular body, tabular numerals everywhere.
- 10px on operable controls, 16px on cards, 20px on sheets and dialogs, 24px on the shell, pills for tags.
- One spring, one curve; selection slides, it never repaints.

## Colors

Apple grey neutrals carry the whole interface; a single indigo accent and the system status hues are the only saturated colors, each held to a job.

### Primary
- **Indigo Ink** (`#533afd` light, `#7f70ff` dark): the accent. Primary buttons, the current selection in lists and menus, checked switches and checkboxes, focus borders, links and text buttons, the filled account control in the top bar, the primary action tile. The dark value is lifted so it holds contrast on black.
- **Indigo Hover** (`#4a34f0` light, `#9d91ff` dark): hover state of every accent surface and link.
- **Periwinkle Wash** (`#efedff` light, `#1f1b3d` dark): the tinted surface behind selected rows, menu selections, info alerts and the mobile tab-bar capsule.
- **Lavender Border** (`#cdc6ff` light, `#4a3fa8` dark) and **Lilac Border** (`#e3dfff`): accent-tinted outlines for selected outline controls and faint accent dividers.
- **Deep Violet** (`#1f1a4d`): text on accent-tinted surfaces, such as info alerts.
- **Amethyst Edge** (`#8b7cff`) and **Indigo Bright** (`#7a66ff`): lighter accent steps for secondary chart series and MUI's `primary.light`.

### Secondary (status)
Apple system hues tuned to 4.5:1 on white in light mode, and the brighter system hues in dark. Used only where meaning demands it: order side, outcome, validation.
- **Buy Green** (`#1d7f39` light, `#30d158` dark): buy side, positive change, success. Filled buy surfaces take white ink in light mode and black ink in dark mode, because white falls to 2:1 on the brighter dark green.
- **Sell Red** (`#d70015` light, `#ff453a` dark): sell side, negative change, errors, destructive actions.
- **Warning Amber** (`#a35200` light, `#ff9f0a` dark): warnings and the ComingSoon notice.
- **Status surfaces**: success `#eef8f0` / `#0c2a16`, danger `#fff1f1` / `#3a1210`, warning `#fff6ea` / `#33230a`. Alert text on these uses the deep steps (`#135c28`, `#a3000f`, `#7a3d00` light; `#6ee38c`, `#ff9a93`, `#ffc061` dark).

### Neutral
- **Shell Ground** (`#f5f5f7`): the ground the shell and every card sit on, and the grouped-sheet background. Dark: **True Black** (`#000000`).
- **Pure White** (`#ffffff`): elevated surfaces: cards, grouped lists, sheets, dialogs, popovers, fields. Dark: **Graphite** (`#1c1c1e`).
- **Frost** (`#e8e8ed`): the default hairline, dividers, hover fill on white. Dark: `#2c2c2e`.
- **Strong Hairline** (`#d2d2d7`): field borders and strong dividers; hover deepens to `#b0b0b8`. Dark: `#3a3a3c`, hover `#48484a`.
- **Control Fill** (`rgba(118, 118, 128, 0.12)`, hover `0.18`; dark `0.24`, hover `0.32`): Apple's translucent system fill. Segmented tracks, the top-nav rail, secondary buttons, search fields, action tiles, avatars and icon-button hovers. Reads on any ground.
- **Midnight Ink** (`#1d1d1f`): primary text. Dark: `#f5f5f7`.
- **Steel** (`#424245`): strong secondary text. Dark: `#98989d`.
- **Slate** (`#66666b`): secondary labels; passes 4.5:1 on white, on the ground, on hover fills and on segmented tracks. Apple's `#6e6e73` falls to 4.15:1 on the `#e8e8ed` hover fill, so slate sits one step darker. Dark: `#aeaeb2`.
- **Smoke** (`#aeaeb2`): tertiary, decorative only (disabled, chevrons, placeholders on large type). Dark: `#636366`.
- **Chrome Material** (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` light, `rgba(28, 28, 30, 0.72)` dark, with `saturate(180%) blur(20px)`): the translucent top bar, mobile header and tab bar.
- **Chart ramp**: indigo ink, `#8e8e93`, `#b3a9ff`, `#48484a`, `#d8d3ff`, deep violet. Charts read as part of the instrument; buy and sell keep their status hues.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Indigo means "act here" or "this is selected". It never decorates, never fills a heading, never tints a card for atmosphere.

**The Meaning-Only Status Rule.** Green and red belong to buy/sell and outcome. A positive number is green because it went up, not because green looks good.

**The Sanctioned Palette Rule.** Every six-digit hex outside the token files must appear in the policy script's palette set. A new color is a system change, not a styling choice. Crypto brand colors (Bridge, Swap, the coins marquee) are the one exemption, kept native.

## Typography

**Display Font:** SF Pro through the system stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "SF Pro Display"`), with self-hosted `Inter Variable` (via `@fontsource-variable/inter`, because the CSP allows only self-hosted fonts) and `system-ui` as fallbacks.
**Body Font:** the same stack.
**Label/Mono Font:** `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, "SF Mono", Menlo`, only for addresses, transaction ids, script source and seed words.

**Character:** One family, one voice. Hierarchy comes from size, weight (400 / 500 / 600) and tracking that tightens as size grows, never from a second face. `"tnum" on, "cv11" on` is set on `:root`, so every figure lines up in its column by default.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, fluid to 56px, 1.05, -1.6px): MUI `h1`. The landing hero pushes the same recipe to `clamp(44px, 7.2vw, 96px)` at -0.03em.
- **Headline Large** (600, fluid to 44px, 1.08, -1.1px): MUI `h2`; the hero balance figure.
- **Headline** (600, fluid to 30px, 1.12, -0.6px): page large titles on desktop ("Welcome back", "Trade", "Settings").
- **Title** (600, fluid to 22px, 1.2, -0.4px): sheet titles, grouped-list headings, section titles.
- **Subheading** (600, 17px, 1.26, -0.3px): card titles, dialog titles.
- **Body Large** (400, 17px, 1.47, -0.3px): grouped-list row titles (at 500), lead paragraphs.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.47, -0.15px): default text and buttons (buttons at 500).
- **Body Small** (400, 13px, 1.43, -0.08px): secondary lines, table cells, alerts, segmented labels (at 500).
- **Caption** (400, 12px, 1.33, 0): chips, tooltips, table heads (at 500), hints under fields.
- **Mobile Large Title** (600, `clamp(30px, 8.7vw, 34px)`, 1.12, -0.02em): the iOS large title that opens every mobile screen.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Truth Rule.** Every price, balance, amount and metric renders in tabular numerals, and every balance and price that changes rolls its digits (AnimatedNumber, 750ms on the system curve) instead of swapping text.

**The Weight Ceiling Rule.** Weights are 400, 500 and 600. 700 is permitted for rare emphasis; 100, 200, 300, 800 and 900 fail the policy check.

**The Sentence-Case Label Rule.** Group headings, column heads and field labels are sentence case in secondary text. There is no uppercase, letter-spaced label above a heading anywhere in the system.

## Layout

A centred content column (max 1320px) under a fixed 64px top bar, with a fluid gutter of `clamp(16px, 4vw, 24px)` and a vertical section gap of `clamp(48px, 8vw, 96px)`. Spacing runs on an 8px base (4px for the tightest gaps; 2px between segments and nav tabs). Every card pads 24px, stepping down with the gutter to 16px; card padding is set once in the theme and pages must not override it with `sx={{ p }}`.

Desktop screens open with a large title and one secondary sentence, then content. The wallet home puts the hero balance card (rolling figure, 1D/1W/1M/1Y segmented control, area chart) across two-thirds and a 2-column grid of action tiles in the right third; assets and recent activity follow as grouped inset lists side by side. The Trade screen is a three-column instrument: chart and your orders on the left, order book and recent trades in the middle, the order ticket on the right. Swap, sign-in and single-task flows centre one card (about 480px) on the ground.

Breakpoints: 600 (sm), 768 (mobile), 1024 (tablet), 1280 (desktop), 1536 (wide); the narrowest supported viewport is 320px. Below 768px the app switches to a separate mobile component tree in the same world: a 44px compact header that collapses from a large title, full-width grouped lists with 16px gutters and 16px card padding, round press actions (Receive, Send, Trade), and a 56px translucent tab bar plus the home-indicator inset. Safe-area insets are applied by the header and tab bar, never by the document.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid: tonal layering does most of the work (white or graphite surfaces on a grey or black ground) and one soft shadow lifts each surface in light mode. Dark mode drops the ladder entirely; surfaces separate by a 1px hairline (`0 0 0 1px` border tone, or a real 1px border), because shadow does not read on black. Only floating layers (dialogs, sheets, toasts, popovers) keep a deep shadow in dark, paired with their hairline.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **sm** (`0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05), 0 1px 1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`): resting cards (MuiCard), the glyph plate inside action tiles.
- **md** (`0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`): grouped inset lists and mobile cards.
- **lg** (`0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`): menus, popovers, bottom sheets.
- **xl** (`0 4px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05), 0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16)`; dark `0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): MUI dialogs.
- **Segment thumb** (`0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 3px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`; dark `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`): the raised pill in segmented controls, tabs and the top-nav rail.
- **Modal panel** (`0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06), 0 28px 56px -24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`; dark hairline plus `0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): the premium ModalSurface.
- **Toast** (`0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`; dark adds a 1px inset top highlight).
- **Focus halo** (`0 0 0 4px rgba(83, 58, 253, 0.18)`; dark `rgba(127, 112, 255, 0.32)`; error `rgba(215, 0, 21, 0.14)`): focused fields and buttons. Global `:focus-visible` draws a 3px outline at `rgba(83, 58, 253, 0.45)` offset 2px.
- **Primary button lift** (`0 1px 2px rgba(83, 58, 253, 0.24)`, light only).

### Named Rules
**The One Elevation Rule.** A surface gets a shadow in light mode or a hairline in dark mode, never both, and never a border plus a shadow at the same time. Light cards carry `1px solid transparent` so their geometry matches dark.

**The Material Is Chrome Rule.** Backdrop blur belongs to chrome and overlays only: the top bar, mobile header, tab bar, landing header once scrolled, modal and sheet backdrops (`blur(6px)` over `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.24)` light, `0.56` dark), tooltips and snackbars. Content surfaces are opaque. The policy check rejects blur anywhere else.

**The Soft Shadow Rule.** Shadows are offset down and diffuse. Hard offset shadows (`Npx Npx 0`) fail the policy check.

## Shapes

Corners come from one numeric scale: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 28, 40 and 9999px. The policy script checks every `border-radius` literal against it. In use: 8px for segment thumbs, menu items, tooltips and skeleton bars; 10px for every operable control (buttons, fields, icon buttons, segmented tracks, command rows); 12px for large buttons, alerts and empty-state tiles; 14px for action tiles, menus, popovers and toasts; 16px for cards, grouped lists and the ComingSoon notice; 20px for dialogs and the top corners of sheets; 24px for the floating shell and landing CTAs; 9999px for chips, status pills and the top-nav rail.

Round shapes (50%) are reserved: asset logos and avatars, glyph plates inside tiles and notices, the close buttons on sheets, dialogs and toasts, and the shell's chrome controls (notifications, settings, account). Operable controls inside a page are rounded rectangles, never circles, which is what separates frame from form at a glance. Hairlines are 1px, and inside grouped lists they are drawn at half a pixel (`scaleY(0.5)`) and inset to start where the text starts.

### Named Rules
**The Scale Is The Statement Rule.** A radius not on the scale is a violation, whatever it looks like.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet, physical and square-shouldered.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px); large buttons 12px.
- **Primary:** indigo fill, white 15px medium label, `10px 20px` padding, 44px minimum height (52px large, 32px small). A faint indigo lift in light mode only.
- **Secondary ("outlined" in MUI):** no border; the translucent control fill with primary-text label, deepening on hover.
- **Text:** indigo label on transparent, control fill on hover.
- **Buy / Sell:** full-width green or red fill with side-appropriate ink (white in light, black in dark).
- **Hover / Focus / Press:** 160ms background tween on the system curve; press scales to 0.97; focus draws the 4px accent halo. No ink ripple anywhere (`disableRipple` is the default).
- **HoldToConfirm:** for irreversible actions a filled copy of the label sweeps across an indigo-tinted face while held; releasing early springs it back. Space or Enter hold it from the keyboard.

### Segmented control (signature)
The system's signature interaction: one raised thumb that springs between options on the shared spring. The track is the control fill (10px, 2px padding); the thumb is white (dark `#636366`, 8px, the segment-thumb shadow). The label under the thumb is redrawn inside it and masked, so its colour changes mid-slide. Labels are 13px medium (12px small). Arrow keys, Home and End move the selection. A `tone` prop tints the thumb green or red for buy/sell. The same pill vocabulary drives MUI Tabs and ToggleButtonGroup (the indicator becomes the raised thumb, 320ms), the desktop top-nav rail (a 9999px track with a `layoutId` pill), the order ticket's quick-amount chips, and the mobile tab bar's periwinkle capsule.

### Rolling numbers
AnimatedNumber wraps `@number-flow/react`: digits roll to their new value over 750ms on the system curve, render as real selectable text in tabular numerals, and default to 2 fraction digits (8 for values under 1). Every balance and price uses it. Figures are never invented: with no series the area chart draws a calm dashed baseline and says so.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px.
- **Background:** pure white on the grey ground; graphite in dark.
- **Shadow Strategy:** `sm` at rest in light, 1px hairline in dark (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** transparent 1px in light, `#2c2c2e` in dark.
- **Internal Padding:** `clamp(16px, 4vw, 24px)`, set by the theme.

### Grouped inset lists
The iOS Settings and Wallet list, in three forms sharing one anatomy: GroupedList, InsetList and SettingsList (plus MarketWatchlist for prices). One 16px surface per group with the `md` shadow (hairline in dark). A group is headed by a sentence-case caption (13px secondary) or a 20px semibold title, and may carry a 13px footer note. Rows are at least 60px (64px in InsetList): a 40px round avatar, a 17px medium title over a 13px secondary line, and the figure right-aligned in tabular numerals, its change tinted by direction. Separators are half-pixel hairlines inset past the avatar. Interactive rows take the control fill on hover and an inset 2px indigo ring on focus. Settings rows put the label left and the control (iOS switch, select, segmented control) right. A sheet on the grouped background lifts its cells through `--grouped-cell` without a prop.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Inset-label field (the form vocabulary):** a 54px field whose label rests inside like a placeholder and springs to the top edge (scale 0.75, 9px up) on focus or once filled, on its own spring (stiffness 760, damping 46, mass 0.5). Height never changes and the field never loses its name. 16px text (15px mono), 10px corners, white surface with a `#d2d2d7` hairline. Password fields carry a show/hide control; multiline becomes a 112px textarea.
- **Style (all fields):** white (translucent fill in dark), strong hairline, 10px corners; MUI OutlinedInput and native selects share the same material through `fieldStyles.ts`.
- **Focus:** the border turns indigo and the soft accent halo appears; no outline.
- **Error / Disabled:** red border with the red halo and a red hint line; disabled drops to 0.55 opacity.
- **AmountField:** after Apple Cash; the figure is the field, centred and oversized, the asset symbol beside it, MAX and the available balance beneath.

### Search field
The only search style in the app: a 40px control-fill well with a 17px glass glyph and no border, so it reads as a filter on the list beneath rather than a form input. 16px text so iOS Safari does not zoom; a round clear button appears once there is a query; focus draws a 3px accent ring.

### Chips and status pills
- **Chips:** 24px (20px small) pills, 12px medium, control-fill background.
- **StatusPill:** a pill with a 7px live dot, the tone at 10% as its background. Only `live` pills ping (the Mainnet pill in the top bar).

### Navigation
- **Desktop top bar:** 64px, chrome material with a 1px bottom hairline. Logo left; the primary destinations centred in a pill rail (13px medium labels, secondary colour, primary when current) with the selection riding a springing white pill; "Everything" opens the launcher from the same rail. Right: the live network pill and 36px round chrome controls, the account control filled indigo.
- **Mobile:** a large-title header collapsing to a 44px bar, and a 56px translucent tab bar with labelled glyphs; the current tab takes the accent and a periwinkle capsule slides between tabs.

### Empty states
Two tiers, deliberately. **EmptyState** is a fanned stack of three 48px icon tiles (12px corners, rotated -6° and 6°) that spread apart on hover, over a 15px semibold title, a 13px secondary line and at most one action; it teaches the next step, and a screen shows it at most once. **PanelEmpty** is the plain form for every other panel: a 14px semibold title and one secondary line, centred, no icon. On Trade, only "Your orders" gets the fan; chart, order book and recent trades use PanelEmpty.

### ComingSoon notice
The gate for a screen that is not live yet, and it comes first: a 16px warning-surface panel (no border, no stripe) with a 32px round amber glyph, a semibold line, a secondary sentence and at most one action on the right. The preview of the unfinished surface sits beneath it, dimmed to 0.6 and `inert`, so it leaves the tab order and the accessibility tree and cannot be mistaken for something that works. Used on Swap, Markets, Messages and Order book.

### Sheets and dialogs
- **Sheet:** rises from the bottom edge on the system spring, max 640px wide, 20px top corners, the `lg` shadow, a 36x5 grab handle, a 22px semibold title. Drag past a third of its height or flick to dismiss; short of that it springs back. Backdrop `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **ModalSurface:** a 20px panel over a blurred, dimmed backdrop. Enters from scale 0.96 and 12px down on a snappy spring (stiffness 420, damping 36); content crossfades on a soft spring (stiffness 260, damping 34); exits as a 150ms tween, because a dismissed thing leaves faster than it arrived. 32px round close control on the control fill.

### Command palette
The "Everything" launcher: a 56px search band (17px input), a scrolling list of destinations under 12px medium group headings, and a key-hint footer, split by hairlines. Rows are 52px with 10px corners and a 32px glyph plate; the highlight slides between rows as arrow keys move it. Focus never leaves the field; the list is announced through `aria-activedescendant`.

### Toast stack
Sonner-style. Toasts are 14px-cornered white cards up to 380px wide with a 22px round type glyph and 14px text. The newest sits in front; older ones tuck behind it, each 5% smaller and 10px lower, so a burst reads as one pile. Hover or focus fans the pile into a list on the system spring; moving away folds it back. Toasts enter from scale 0.96, 16px above their slot; exit a 150ms fade and shrink. The ToastContext host owns timing and announcements.

### Action tiles and press actions
- **ActionTile:** a 72px tile on the control fill (14px corners, 12px padding), a 28px round glyph plate over a 14px medium label; springs to 0.97 on press. The primary tile (Send) is filled indigo with white ink.
- **PressAction (mobile):** a round periwinkle-tinted action with its label beneath, the whole column the tap target; the circle sinks and leans toward the finger on press.

### Trading parts
The order ticket stacks a Buy/Sell segmented control (thumb tinted by side), a Limit/Market control, fields with the unit inside, quick-amount chips (25/50/75/100%), a quiet summary and one full-width side-coloured action. PriceLadder folds a depth chart into the order book rows (green and red at low opacity). TradeTape slides new fills in from the top with one fading tone-tinted wash, never a flash.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every value from `src/styles/tokens.ts` (or its mirrors: the `index.css` custom properties, the styled-components themes, the MUI theme). Nothing is hand-tuned per page.
- **Do** keep indigo for primary action, current selection, focus and links, and nothing else.
- **Do** give a surface a shadow in light mode or a hairline in dark mode, never both.
- **Do** use the shared spring (stiffness 520, damping 38, mass 0.8) for anything that slides, presses, stacks or rises, and `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for tweens (160 / 240 / 420ms). Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; every premium component drops to an instant change.
- **Do** render every figure in tabular numerals, and roll balances and prices with AnimatedNumber.
- **Do** use SegmentedControl (or the themed MUI Tabs, which render the same pill) for every two-to-five-way choice.
- **Do** build forms from InsetLabelField, searches from SearchField, and lists from the grouped inset list.
- **Do** use the fanned EmptyState at most once per screen and PanelEmpty everywhere else; put a ComingSoon notice first and render the unfinished preview `inert` beneath it.
- **Do** keep tap targets at 44px or more and radii on the scale.
- **Do** run `npm run policy:design` before shipping; it must pass.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use an uppercase, letter-spaced eyebrow or kicker above a heading. Labels are sentence case.
- **Don't** use gradient text or decorative gradients. Gradients are allowed only for skeleton shimmer, image placeholders and chart fills that fade a series to nothing (the policy exempts `components/premium/`, `components/charts/`, skeletons and LazyImage for exactly this).
- **Don't** lay decorative grid, dot or noise overlays over the ground.
- **Don't** put a coloured side-stripe border on cards, rows, alerts or notices. Selection is a tinted wash or a raised pill.
- **Don't** stack a border and a shadow on one surface, or use hard offset shadows.
- **Don't** blur content surfaces; blur is chrome material and overlay backdrops only.
- **Don't** use weights 100, 200, 300, 800 or 900, or a second typeface.
- **Don't** introduce a hex outside the policy palette, or a radius outside the scale.
- **Don't** use emoji; use the Icon component.
- **Don't** add an ink ripple; presses answer with scale and tint.
- **Don't** draw a chart, trend or metric the data does not have. With no series, show the baseline and say so.
